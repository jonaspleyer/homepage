This paper uses `cellular_raza` to develop a model with cell-type specific interactions whereby
cells self-assemble into regions of similar species which is also known as cell-sorting.
We use this model to asses the parallelization performance of the numerical backend at the core of
cellular_raza and show that values of up to $p=97.78\pm 0.14\\%$ parallelizable code can be achieved,
which indicates a high level of parallelizability.
